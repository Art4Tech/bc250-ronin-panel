# Ronin October 6 hardware illustrations

These are Blender renders from the October 6 working CAD and the Gerbers used for the ordered Rev A PCB. They are design visualizations, not photographs of manufactured hardware.

- [Adapter top](ronin-led-adapter-top.jpg) and [detail](ronin-led-adapter-detail.jpg): current component bodies and ordered copper/mask/silkscreen. Some IC/fuse packages are simplified, and the single 5 V selector shunt is illustrative rather than a confirmed shipment configuration.
- [Adapter back](ronin-led-adapter-bottom.jpg): actual ordered exposed-copper logo and board-name artwork, with an ENIG material.
- [NVMe locator](ronin-nvme-route.jpg): current modeled extension, relocated 2280 SSD, two printed mounts and ribbon route. Pink is the ribbon, lavender the mounts, and teal the SSD/extension. Ghosted backbone/BC250 geometry makes the route visible. Colors do not describe actual material, temperature or airflow.

Board arrival, assembly inspection, electrical/thermal checks, installed fit and LED control remain pending. The cable drawing is not a cut-length, bend-limit or PCIe validation. The public CAD/software packages elsewhere in the repository remain earlier snapshots; see the manual revision notes and current display status.

Native PCB/CAD manufacturing inputs, Gerbers, BOM/CPL and Blender source files are withheld from this public media update.
