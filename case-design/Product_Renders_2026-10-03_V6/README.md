# V6c — The Computer They Didn't Make

[Watch the 89-second film](https://media.githubusercontent.com/media/Art4Tech/bc250-ronin-panel/main/case-design/Product_Renders_2026-10-03_V6/BC250_The_Computer_They_Didnt_Make_V6c.mp4) · [Case study](https://artfedderson.tech/work/bc250-ronin) · [Lighting revision](https://artfedderson.tech/blog/bc250-matchstick-lighting-update)

Published media revision, October 3, 2026. 1920 × 1080, 24 fps, 89 seconds, H.264 with stereo AAC and fast-start metadata. Instrumental score; no narration, typing clicks or abrasive transition wipes. Twenty seconds of additional reading time are distributed across the captions. The final card uses “soon™.”

The current lighting assembly uses two Rainbow on a Matchstick boards by Blamm, ten RGB LEDs each, and their printed diffusers in the updated CAD positions. The earlier rings and foot strip have been removed. Transparent 3D-Fuel PCTG is visualized with frosted surfaces and visible 0.20 mm FDM layer lines; color comes from the RGB LEDs.

The case and its contents are native CAD renders. The maker-desk scenes combine them and official Voron V0.2r1 CAD with generated room/person backgrounds. This is an independent, unofficial thought experiment with no Voron endorsement. Images do not show a fabricated enclosure or establish performance, fit or thermal results. Enclosure fabrication, revised lighting integration and gamepad startup remain unfinished.

Specific contributor credits: Rainbow on a Matchstick by Blamm; EPS Power Adapter and PS_ON adapter by Mosfet.Party; the single 92 mm fan-shroud remix based on MandicReally's CAD.

This folder publishes the final film, its verification report, and compressed website imagery. It does not replace the original SolidWorks snapshot or claim to be a new Pack and Go export. Earlier V5 films and editable scenes retain their original paths. The new editable Blender masters and complete companion assets are in the local V6 delivery.
